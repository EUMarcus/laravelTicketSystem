## 🎨 Design Principles Applied

### 1. **Reduced Color Overload**
- ✅ Removed excessive green usage
- ✅ Limited color usage to primary and accents only
- ✅ Badges use subtle beige/tan instead of bright colors
- ✅ Status indicators are more muted

### 2. **Cleaner Color Application**
- ✅ Primary color (#5D866C) used sparingly for:
  - Main buttons
  - Active links
  - Important status indicators
- ✅ Accent colors (tan/beige) used for:
  - Category badges
  - Subtle highlights
  - Background accents
- ✅ Gray tones for:
  - Secondary text
  - Muted elements
  - Borders

### 3. **Professional Aesthetic**
- ✅ Soft, muted palette
- ✅ No harsh black colors
- ✅ Cream backgrounds for warmth
- ✅ Subtle contrasts
- ✅ Clean, minimal design

## 📋 Color Usage Guide

### Use Primary Green (#5D866C) For:
- Primary buttons
- Active navigation items
- Important status (Open, Active)
- Links and call-to-actions
- Brand elements

### Use Accent Colors For:
- **Tan (#C2A68C)**: Secondary buttons, warnings
- **Beige (#E6D8C3)**: Category badges, subtle backgrounds
- **Cream (#F5F5F0)**: Main page backgrounds

### Use Grays For:
- Secondary text (#4a5568)
- Muted text (#718096)
- Borders and dividers
- Disabled states
